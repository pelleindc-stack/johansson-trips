Keep handy

Yellowstone safety

  • Wildlife: stay at least 100 yards from bears and wolves; 25 yards from other wildlife.
  • Thermal areas: stay on boardwalks and marked trails.
  • Bear country: carry bear spray when hiking. Buy after landing—do not attempt to fly with it.
  • Driving: expect wildlife jams, allow extra time and fuel up before long park days.
  • Offline: Apple Maps area and Yellowstone NPS app are already downloaded.

Friends’ Yellowstone Tips

Worth prioritizing

  • Elk herd near Mammoth Hot Springs and the hot springs.
  • Lamar Valley around 5 AM for wolves and other wildlife; it is also excellent around dusk.
  • Mount Washburn for bighorn sheep; Dunraven Pass has frequent grizzly sightings.
  • Norris Geyser Basin sulfuric pools and the North Rim Trail at Grand Canyon of Yellowstone.
  • Old Faithful is worth seeing once despite being touristy; the Old Faithful Inn is worth seeing too.
  • Yellowstone Lake Hotel is beautiful and old-time elegant if route and time allow.
  • Roosevelt Lodge horseback riding / cowboy cookout is a good future-trip idea.

Jackson Hole / Persephone Bakery are intentionally outside this trip’s core route.
